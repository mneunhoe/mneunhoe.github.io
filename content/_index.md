Welcome! I'm a computational social scientist and statistician working at the intersection of statistics, machine learning, and data privacy. My research asks one question: how do we do valid statistical inference on data that an upstream process, more and more often an AI model, has already transformed? I study four instances of this problem, missing-data imputation, synthetic data, differential privacy, and predictions from pretrained tabular foundation models.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [Institute for Employment Research (IAB)](https://iab.de/mitarbeiter/neunhoeffer-marcel/) and a Teaching and Research Associate at [LMU Munich](https://www.soda.statistik.uni-muenchen.de/index.html). I have worked on privacy-preserving methods for official statistics with the US Census Bureau and the German Federal Statistical Office.

My work emphasizes rigorous evaluation over hype - demonstrating both the potential and limitations of modern machine learning methods for social science applications.
